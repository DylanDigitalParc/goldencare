About Me

Meet Steve Loveless

With 26 years of experience in the insurance industry, I’m proud to serve clients in 37 different states across the country through GoldenCare USA—a nationally recognized leader in long-term care insurance. I began my career at age 20, and since joining GoldenCare in 2006, I’ve specialized in Long-Term Care (LTC) insurance, Extended Care, Annuities, and comprehensive retirement planning for families.

My focus is always on making insurance easy to understand and delivering peace of mind. I’m dedicated to providing solutions tailored to each client’s unique needs, leveraging GoldenCare’s wide access to top-rated insurance carriers.

This flexibility ensures I can offer the most suitable and cost-effective programs available. Building lasting relationships with my clients is my priority.

I’m here to guide you every step of the way—before, during, and long after your policy is in place. My goal is to provide honest advice, clear choices, and ongoing support, so you always feel confident and cared for.

Outside of work, I cherish time with my wife of 16 years, Carrie, and our three children—Landon, Ella, and Braxton. If you’re looking for guidance in protecting your future, I’m here to help every step of the way.
